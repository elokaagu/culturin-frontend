# Culturin Brand Guide

Version 1.0 · 2026

A working guide for anyone creating Culturin assets: designers, partners, and AI tools like ChatGPT and Midjourney. Section 8 has copy-and-paste prompts.

## 1. Who we are

**Culturin connects the world through culture.** We're a network of founders, operators, artists, and cultural leaders. We build rooms at the pinnacle of culture (Cannes Lions, UNGA, the US Open, Frieze, Art Basel) and help brands enter new territories with cultural intelligence.


|                      |                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tagline**          | We connect the world through Culture.                                                                                               |
| **Positioning line** | We create iconic moments at the pinnacle of culture.                                                                                |
| **What we sell**     | Intelligence · Programming · Moments                                                                                                |

**Brand personality:** warm, discerning, well-connected, understated, nocturnal. Think of the best host you know: they never announce how good the party is, they just make sure the right people meet.

## 2. Voice and tone



### Principles

1. **Say it plainly.** Short sentences. Concrete nouns. No jargon.
2. **Confident, never loud.** We don't shout "exclusive" or "world-class". We let the rooms, names, and places do the work.
3. **Human first.** Talk about people, rooms, nights, and conversations, not "activations", "touchpoints" or "synergies".
4. **Specific beats superlative.** "500 guests from 12 countries" beats "a huge international crowd".
5. **Warm, with a little wit.** "Cannes, without the noise."



### House style

- **British English:** programme, colour, centre, organise.
- **No em dashes.** Use commas, colons, or full stops.
- **Headlines in sentence case**, often ending with a full stop: "Life inside the rooms."
- **Eyebrow labels in small caps with wide letter-spacing:** WHAT WE DO, WHERE WE'LL BE.
- **Write "Culturin", never "CULTURIN" in body copy.** The all-caps form is reserved for the logo.
- Numbers: numerals for stats (500+, 12 countries), words for one to nine in prose.



### Our words


| Use                            | Avoid                                         |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| rooms, nights, moments         | activations, touchpoints                      |
| guests, the people in the room | consumers, targets, users                     |
| woven in with intention        | integrated, leveraged                         |
| curated guest list             | exclusive, VIP-only                           |
| culture, taste, community      | lifestyle, content (as a noun for everything) |
| partners                       | sponsors (unless it's contractual)            |




### Example lines

- "We put the right people in the same room."
- "Launch in a new territory with the room already built."
- "La Croisette. Our way."
- "Brands come to us for cultural marketing."

## 3. Logo

The Culturin logo has two parts:

- **The mark:** an eight-point burst. Eight rounded, arrow-shaped petals point inward to a shared centre. It stands for people arriving from every direction into one room.
- **The wordmark:** CULTURIN in Recoleta, all caps, with a small superscript ™.



### Versions and files


| Version                          | Use on                                | File                                        |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Mark, yellow                     | Dark backgrounds and photography      | `public/logo/culturin_logo_icon_yellow.png` |
| Mark, black                      | Cream and light backgrounds           | `public/logo/culturin_logo_icon_black.png`  |
| Mark, white                      | Busy photography where yellow clashes | `public/logo/culturin_logo_icon_white.png`  |
| Wordmark, yellow / black / white | Same rules as the mark                | `public/logo/culturin_logotype_*.png`       |
| Full logo (vector)               | Print, large formats                  | `public/culturin_logo_black.svg`            |




### Rules

- **Clear space:** keep at least the height of one petal clear around the logo on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** 20px (digital) or 8mm (print) for the mark.
- **Colour:** logo yellow (#FAC100) on dark, ink (#1C1A17) on light. Never terracotta, never gradients.
- **Don't:** stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, recolour the petals individually, or place it over a busy part of a photo without enough contrast.
- **Don't** ask AI tools to draw the logo. Generate the image, then place the real logo file on top.

## 4. Colour

The palette is warm and nocturnal: candlelight on cream stone, terracotta, late-night ink.

### Core palette


| Name            | Hex       | Role                                                              |
| --------------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ink**         | `#1C1A17` | Primary dark. Text on light, the "room at night" background.      |
| **Night**       | `#17130F` | Deepest background (dark mode).                                   |
| **Cream**       | `#E8E3DA` | Primary light background.                                         |
| **Parchment**   | `#F1E9DC` | Text on dark backgrounds.                                         |
| **Terracotta**  | `#B5502E` | Accent on light backgrounds: highlighted words, buttons, numbers. |
| **Copper Glow** | `#E08A5B` | Accent on dark backgrounds: buttons, highlighted words, stats.    |
| **Logo Yellow** | `#FAC100` | Logo only. Never used for text or UI.                             |




### Supporting neutrals


| Name  | Hex       | Role                    |
| ----- | --------- | ----------------------- |
| Stone | `#6B6456` | Secondary text on cream |
| Sand  | `#B9AB98` | Secondary text on dark  |
| Linen | `#CEC7BE` | Hairline rules on cream |
| Umber | `#3A332A` | Hairline rules on dark  |




### Proportions

Roughly **70% Ink or Cream**, **25% photography**, **5% accent**. The accent is a spotlight, not a paint bucket. Use it for one highlighted word, one button, or one number per layout.

### Pairings that work

- Parchment text on Ink, with a Copper Glow button.
- Ink text on Cream, with one italic word in Terracotta.
- Full-bleed photography with a dark gradient at the bottom and Parchment text over it.

## 5. Typography


| Role                      | Typeface                               | Free alternative for Canva, Figma, and AI mockups |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Display and headlines** | **Recoleta** (Medium 500)              | Fraunces (soft, low contrast)                     |
| **Body, labels, buttons** | **TWK Everett** (Book 400, Medium 500) | Inter or Manrope                                  |




### Hierarchy

- **Headline:** Recoleta Medium, tight line height (1.05–1.1), sentence case, often with one word in *italic* and accent colour. Example: "We create *iconic moments* at the pinnacle of culture."
- **Eyebrow:** Everett Semibold, 10–11px, ALL CAPS, letter-spacing 0.3em, in Stone or Sand.
- **Body:** Everett Book, 15–16px, generous line height (1.7–1.9), Stone on cream or Sand on dark.
- **Buttons:** Everett Semibold, 11–12px, ALL CAPS, letter-spacing 0.18em, in a fully rounded pill.
- **Big stats:** Recoleta Medium, very large, in the accent colour. Example: **61%**.

## 6. Photography and art direction

Our imagery should feel like **being inside the room at 11pm**: warm, candid, intimate, alive.

### Always

- **Real people** mid-conversation, laughing, dancing, embracing. Diverse, stylish, confident.
- **Warm light:** tungsten, candlelight, red and amber club lighting, golden hour on the Riviera.
- **Candid, documentary energy:** direct flash at night, slight motion blur, natural film grain.
- **Texture:** velvet, brass, linen tablecloths, glassware, disco balls, terracotta walls.
- **Places with character:** Riviera terraces, members' clubs, galleries, rooftops, private dining rooms.



### Never

- Stock-looking poses, forced smiles at camera, or corporate headshots.
- Cold blue or clinical lighting, empty venues, sterile conference halls.
- Heavy filters, HDR, oversaturation, or obviously AI-smooth skin.
- Visible competitor logos, or recognisable celebrities in AI-generated images.



### Colour grade

Warm shadows (brown-black, never blue-black), creamy highlights, rich reds and ambers, lifted blacks, subtle grain.

## 7. Layout and graphic language

- **Generous space.** Let headlines breathe. Big margins.
- **Rounded corners:** 16–32px radius on images and cards. Buttons are always full pills.
- **Hairline rules** (1px Linen or Umber) separate sections. No heavy boxes or drop shadows.
- **Numbering:** 01, 02, 03 in small accent-coloured caps for services, steps, and chapters.
- **Lower-third text:** on hero images, the headline sits bottom-left and supporting text bottom-right, over a dark gradient.
- **One italic accent word** per headline, maximum.
- **Motion** (web and video): slow, eased fade-and-rise reveals. Nothing bouncy or flashy.



### Standard asset sizes


| Asset                     | Size                      |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Instagram post            | 1080 × 1350 (4:5)         |
| Instagram / TikTok story  | 1080 × 1920 (9:16)        |
| LinkedIn post             | 1200 × 1200 or 1200 × 627 |
| Website social share (OG) | 1200 × 630                |
| Email header              | 1200 × 600                |
| Presentation slide        | 1920 × 1080 (16:9)        |

## 8. AI prompt kit



### 8.1 ChatGPT: brand voice instructions

Paste this into ChatGPT **Custom Instructions**, a **Project**, or a custom **GPT**:

```text
You are writing for Culturin, a cultural marketing company and network of founders, operators, artists, and cultural leaders. Tagline: "We connect the world through Culture." Culturin builds rooms at the pinnacle of culture (Cannes Lions, UNGA, the US Open, Frieze, Art Basel) and helps brands enter new territories with cultural intelligence. Services: Intelligence (ongoing cultural insight), Programming (a year of rooms built around a brand), Moments (sponsoring a room Culturin has already built). Proof: the team has produced cultural moments at the Super Bowl, the Oscars, Davos, the Cannes Film Festival, and the UN General Assembly.

Voice: warm, discerning, understated, confident but never loud. Like a great host who never brags about the party.

Rules:
- British English (programme, colour, centre).
- Never use em dashes. Use commas, colons, or full stops.
- Short sentences, plain words, concrete details. Specific beats superlative.
- Headlines in sentence case, often ending in a full stop.
- Say "rooms", "nights", "moments", "guests", "partners", "woven in with intention".
- Never say "activation", "touchpoint", "synergy", "leverage", "world-class", "exclusive", "VIP", "unlock", "elevate", "game-changer".
- No emojis unless asked. No hashtags unless asked.
- Write "Culturin", never "CULTURIN", in body copy.

Example lines: "We put the right people in the same room." / "Launch in a new territory with the room already built." / "La Croisette. Our way." / "Cannes, without the noise."
```

**Useful ChatGPT requests once that's set up:**

- "Write three Instagram captions for our [event] recap. Under 40 words each."
- "Write a LinkedIn post announcing Culturin at [event], [dates], [city]. End with a soft call to action."
- "Write an email to our list about [event]. Subject line options, preview text, then a body under 150 words."
- "Rewrite this in the Culturin voice: [paste text]."



### 8.2 Midjourney: house style

Add this **style suffix** to the end of any Midjourney prompt:

```text
candid documentary event photography, warm tungsten and candlelight, deep amber and red tones, brown-black shadows, creamy highlights, subtle film grain, shot on 35mm, direct flash, intimate, stylish, diverse guests, editorial, Kinfolk meets Apartamento --style raw --ar 4:5 --s 150
```

Add this **negative prompt** to stay on-brand:

```text
--no text, logos, watermark, cold blue light, stock photo, posed smiles, HDR, oversaturated, plastic skin, empty room
```

**Aspect ratios:** `--ar 4:5` for Instagram posts, `--ar 9:16` for stories, `--ar 16:9` for web heroes and slides, `--ar 3:2` for email headers.

### 8.3 Midjourney: prompt templates

Replace the parts in [brackets], then add the style suffix and negative prompt above.

**Event atmosphere (hero images)**

```text
a candlelit private dinner on a Riviera terrace at night, long table with linen and glassware, creative industry guests in conversation and laughter, [city] lights in the background
```

**Nightlife energy**

```text
a packed intimate members' club at midnight, red and amber lighting, disco ball reflections, stylish diverse crowd dancing, DJ booth glowing in the background
```

**Conversations and connection (B2B)**

```text
two founders mid-conversation at a cocktail party, one laughing, holding coupe glasses, warm bokeh lights behind, gallery walls with contemporary art
```

**Place-led (destination teasers)**

```text
golden hour on La Croisette in Cannes, palm trees, a terrace set for an evening event, terracotta and cream tones, anticipation before guests arrive
```

**Detail and texture (backgrounds for text)**

```text
close-up still life of a dinner table after midnight, half-melted candles, wine glasses, scattered flowers, crumpled linen, deep shadows, lots of negative space on the left
```

**Abstract brand background**

```text
soft abstract gradient of warm terracotta, copper and deep ink brown, velvety texture, subtle grain, like light through a red curtain, minimal, lots of negative space
```

**Tips**

- Ask for **negative space** ("lots of empty space on the left" or "at the top") when you plan to add text.
- Use `--sref` with a URL of one of our real event photos to lock in the look across a series.
- Never generate real people's likenesses, celebrities, or other brands' logos.
- Midjourney can't spell reliably. **Add all text and the logo afterwards** in Canva or Figma, using the fonts in section 5.



### 8.4 ChatGPT image generation: layouts with text

ChatGPT's image generator handles text better, so use it for quick social graphic drafts. Still place the real logo afterwards.

```text
Design a 4:5 Instagram post for Culturin. Background: a warm, candid night-time photo of stylish guests at an intimate event, dark gradient at the bottom. Bottom-left, a headline in a soft, rounded serif similar to Recoleta, in warm off-white #F1E9DC: "[Headline]." Make one word, "[word]", italic and in copper #E08A5B. Above the headline, a tiny letter-spaced all-caps label in sans-serif: "[EYEBROW]". Bottom-right, a small fully rounded pill button in #E08A5B with dark text "[BUTTON TEXT]". Leave the top-left corner empty for a logo. Minimal, editorial, lots of breathing room. No other text.
```

**Event announcement card**

```text
A 1080x1350 announcement card on a deep ink background #1C1A17. Large soft serif headline in #F1E9DC: "Culturin at [Event]." Beneath it in small letter-spaced caps: "[DATES] · [CITY]". A thin 1px line in #3A332A separates a short line of body text in a clean sans-serif: "[One-sentence description]." Generous margins, editorial, minimal. No logos, no icons, no extra text.
```

**Stat card (for reports and LinkedIn)**

```text
A minimal square LinkedIn graphic on warm cream #E8E3DA. One huge number "[61%]" in a soft rounded serif, terracotta #B5502E. Beneath it, one sentence in a clean dark sans-serif #1C1A17: "[of consumers are more inclined to buy after a live brand experience.]" A tiny source line at the bottom in grey #6B6456: "Source: [source]". Lots of empty space. No other elements.
```

## 9. Before you publish: checklist

- [ ] Real logo file placed, not AI-generated, with clear space respected.
- [ ] Colours from the palette only. One accent moment per layout.
- [ ] Headline in Recoleta (or Fraunces), body in Everett (or Inter).
- [ ] Copy is British English, has no em dashes, and avoids the words we don't use.
- [ ] Photography feels warm, candid, and human, not stock or AI-glossy.
- [ ] Any stat has a named source.
- [ ] Sized correctly for the channel (section 7).
